How statuses work
Status is read from the folder name. Put a tag anywhere in the name: [DONE], [PERMIT], [REVIEW], [HOLD]. No tag = Active. You can also override it on any card; overrides are saved on this device.

Name folders like 1234 Main St – Smith ADU [PERMIT] and the address becomes the title and the client the subtitle.
