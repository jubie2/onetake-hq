## Verdict

- **No investment-ready deal today.** 0 of 53 live properties make money at asking price on first-time-builder financing.
- **6 of the 8 best-looking leads already sold**: Natchez $970K, Marengo $801.6K (14.5% over asking), Covina $1.05M (16% over), Fermi $830K, 4575 Dwight $570K, Skyline $500K (13% under asking, probate).
- **Profit is made on the purchase.** Buy rule: max offer = 70% of sold-comp finished value minus repairs. Listed fixers trade at 80–100%+ of finished value.
- **Adding an ADU to a flip never paid.** Six options (garage conversion, JADU, detached 499/750/1,000/1,200 sf) were tested on every house; all lowered profit, smaller lost less. A 750 sf ADU costs ~$370K all-in and adds ~$125–225K to resale. ADUs work as holds (~8–11% gross yield), not flips.
- **New construction on land loses everywhere tested.** Break-even resale: 1105 Eucalyptus $1,331/sf, 0 Cushman $1,078/sf, Trenton $1,144/sf, 0 Dwight $832/sf, Amalfi $1,960/sf. Sold evidence: Oceanside $745–784/sf, Vista $450–532/sf, Carlsbad new townhomes $591–632/sf.
- **SB 9 lot splits are barred for investors** (3-year owner-occupancy affidavit; AB 2005 fix died). **SB 1123** (vacant SF lot ≤1.5 ac → up to 10 for-sale homes, ministerial, no owner-occupancy) is the scalable path, but the Carlsbad lot failed (likely Coastal Zone, possible unrecorded lot split, comps 25% below assumption).
- **Best-fit financing is owner-occupied FHA 203(k)/HomeStyle** (~$40–60K cash, rehab financed, Sec. 121 exclusion after 2 years). First-time ground-up loans need completed projects or an experienced GC/guarantor.

| Owner-builder | B&P §7044(a)(2): up to 4 for-sale houses/yr contracting directly with licensed trades | Legal path for Dad to run builds |

## Assumptions (first-time builder, base case)

Rehab $50/$110/$200 per sf + $15K systems allowance · detached ADU $340–425/sf + $36–85K soft · new house $400/sf inland ($650 La Jolla) + sprinklers + separate grading · contingency 15% (20% hillside land) · hard money 11.5% + 2.5 pts + fees, loan ≤ 70% of value (flips) / ≤ 60% of value (land, no build interest during permitting) · tax 1.2% + supplemental, insurance ≥ $350/mo · buy 1.5% / sell 6.5% (8% conservative). Conservative: value −7% (land −13%), costs +15%, +2 months (land +6), 12.5% money. Optimistic: value +5%, costs −5%, −1 month on flips. Risk-adjusted = weighted scenarios, weight shifting to conservative per risk flag. Experienced-operator terms (10%, 1.5 pts, 10% contingency, 5.5% selling) improve flips by only $25–60K.
