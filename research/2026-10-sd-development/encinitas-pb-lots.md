## Takeaway
Land under $500K in these areas exists only as problem lots: hillside, missing utilities, constrained zoning. Whether either one works depends on what the site work really costs. That is an engineering question, so it's where Dad's edge applies. Both projects need ~$0.9–1.25M of cash (JV or owner-occupied construction loan); a first-time ground-up lender won't fund them alone.
