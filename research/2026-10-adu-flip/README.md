## Verdict

- **Nothing is a buy at asking price.** 5 of 40 show any profit; the best is +$82k (3115 Natchez Ave duplex, status stale since June).
- **Adding a detached ADU cuts a flip's profit by $195k–$260k.** It costs ~$354k all-in (750 sf at $375/sf + 10% contingency + $45k soft) plus 7 extra months of 11% interest, and adds only ~$150–300k to resale.
- **All 9 land deals lose money** as new builds at asking price, from La Jolla (−$1.5M) to City Heights (−$355k).
- **Profit comes from the buy price:** to net $100k, buy at ~65–70% of resale value (cosmetic), ~60–65% (medium), ~50% (heavy). Most asks here are 70–90%.
- **Market proof:** 825 51st St sold ~$816k (Aug 2026) despite permitted plans for 6 ADUs, roughly house-only value. 2845 Arcola Ave (Bay Park "major fixer") listed at $1.048M and sold for $1.168M.

## Rules that move the numbers

- **AB 1033 (sell ADU as condo):** City of SD (outside Coastal Zone, since Aug 2025; HCD flagged O-21989 non-compliant Oct 2025) and unincorporated County (Ord. 10986, since 2026-04-04). Not adopted: Oceanside, Vista, La Mesa, Lemon Grove, El Cajon. No SD County ADU-condo sale on record; only CA comp is San Jose, 749 sf, $530k (June 2026).
- **City of SD ADU Bonus (O-21989):** banned in RS-1-1–4 and RS-1-8–11; RS-1-5/6/7 still eligible; 4–6 unit caps by lot size; affordable deed restrictions make it a hold strategy.
- **SB 9 lot split:** 3-year owner-occupancy affidavit → not usable on a flip.
- **Fees/timing:** no impact fees under 750 sf; SDUSD school fee $4.79/sf over 500 sf; separate water meter $13–25k; SDG&E new service 82–266 days; City ADU permit ~4–6 months; only 43% of detached ADUs reach final inspection within 3 years.

## Caveats

- WebFetch was blocked in the research environment, so prices and statuses come from search-result snippets of the linked pages; several are months old. Check live MLS status before any offer.
- Zoning, slope, fire zone and transit status were not confirmed parcel by parcel. Run each address through ZAPP / SanGIS.
- ARVs are estimates from the nearest 2025–26 sales found; cards marked "assumed" use guessed square footage.
- Not every earlier shortlist property could be identified. All named addresses are covered, plus the best active listings found per ZIP.
